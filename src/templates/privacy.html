Privacy Policy

Please read this page in conjunction with our terms and conditions.

Email

  • A valid email address might be required to sign up for an account.
  • We use your email address to send you important information about AnkiCollab and your account, such as a reset link when you forget your password.
  • We will never use your email for marketing emails or sell your data.

User Data

  • When you add cards via AnkiCollab, or use the synchronization feature in the Anki clients to send your data to AnkiCollab, we store the card data, associated media files, (optionally) your review history, which is linked to your account, the IP Address used to upload the data, your username and device information.
  • When you answer the review-statistics prompt, we store the decision itself: the answer, the date and time, the version of the wording you were shown, and a hash of your account. We keep this so we can demonstrate that consent was given, and for no other purpose.
  • If you set up a passkey, we store the public key and credential identifier that your device creates, a name for it, and when it was last used. Your fingerprint, face or device PIN stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • This personal data is not visible to other users of the service, with the single exception described in the next point.
  • Contribution records are a distinct, narrower category of data. When you suggest a change to a deck, we record the suggestion, its outcome (accepted, or reviewed with feedback given), and the public username that is already shown on suggestions you submit. These records are visible to the deck's maintainers, so they can credit the people who improved the deck. A per-contributor breakdown is only shown once at least 5 people have contributed to that deck, so no individual's contribution pattern can be picked out; below that, only totals are shown.
  • Contribution records also name the maintainer who accepted a suggestion or gave feedback on it. Deck maintainers see this as a count per person, and a contributor is told who handled their own suggestion ("accepted by alice on 6 October 2026"). We did not record this before 8 October 2026, so older reviews show as "not recorded" rather than being attributed to anyone.
  • This data is visible to the dev team when investigating problems with the service, and problems you report.
  • In the interests of research and product development, we may use your review history, content and options for statistical purposes, such as calculating the average pass rate across all users.

Cookies

  • AnkiCollab uses an internet technology called "cookies" to keep you logged in to the website and necessary cookies set by the Provider Cloudflare to protect against malicious traffic and reduce load times of the website.
  • The Cookies used by cloudflare can be found here: https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/
  • The cookies allow us to associate the actions you take with the correct account.
  • Cloudflare may process data on servers outside the EU under Standard Contractual Clauses in accordance with GDPR Article 46.
  • AnkiCollab does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Logs and Error Reporting

  • All requests made to AnkiCollab, whether they be through a web browser or through one of the Anki clients, are logged.
  • We record the date and time, the page on AnkiCollab that is being accessed, IP address and device information.
  • When you access the service while logged in, your user id is recorded.
  • Email-related operations may log your email address.
  • We store this information for security purposes (such as blocking malicious access attempts), for diagnosing problems, for analyzing usage of the service, and for providing support.
  • Logs and error reports are automatically sent and stored via secure means to our infrastructure.

Syncing

  • When you use the synchronization feature to transfer data between an Anki client and AnkiCollab, the client sends the hashed names of the subscribed decks, the timestamp of your last synchronization, device information (such as unique identifiers or user tokens), and, if you have opted in for that deck, your review history.
  • AnkiCollab does not store the deck contents exchanged during a synchronization request for longer than it needs to process that request.
  • Review history is only sent if you opt in, and you opt in per deck. The add-on asks you the first time a deck would share statistics, and you can change your answer at any time under Manage Subscriptions → ⋯ → Configure → Review data for that deck.
  • When you opt in, the add-on sends per-note retention, lapse and repetition counts (averaged per deck), the names of the decks and subdecks you reviewed in, and an identifier for each note. It is sent with your access token, so it is linked to your AnkiCollab account: the deck's maintainers see the statistics, not your username, but the data is not anonymous and we can identify which account uploaded it.
  • We keep the statistics for as long as you share them. Turning sharing off stops future uploads immediately and schedules the statistics you have already shared for deletion; the deletion is complete within 24 hours, and cached copies of statistics pages may take up to that long to disappear. If you remove a subscription, we offer to delete them at the same time. Erased data no longer contributes to the deck's statistics from that point on.
  • As part of your subscription to a deck, we record the time of your last sync of that deck. This is what lets a deck report how many of its subscribers are active, for example "12 subscribers active in the last 30 days". It is only ever reported to the deck's maintainers as a count, never as a list of who synced when.
  • Your AnkiCollab login token is stored locally in the addon's configuration file inside Anki's data folder on your device, so that you don't have to log in again every time you sync. Anyone with access to your computer's user account could potentially read this file, so keep your device secured the same way you would for any other locally stored credential.

User Support

  • For support requests, please join our discord server.

Review statistics and your rights

Review statistics are entirely opt-in and are never a condition of using AnkiCollab.

  • Withdraw: Manage Subscriptions → ⋯ → Configure → Review data, per deck. You can also do this when you remove a subscription.
  • Effect: future uploads stop immediately, and we schedule the stats you already shared for deletion. The deletion is carried out within 24 hours, after which the data no longer appears in deck statistics; cached pages may take up to that long to refresh.
  • Refuse: saying no once is enough; the add-on will not ask again. You can change your mind later from the same settings screen.
  • Other rights: access, rectification, restriction, objection, portability, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Contact us via Discord or the contact route already listed on this page.

Locations

  • We use servers and cloud services located inside secure data centers in the European Union to store your data.
  • Portions of AnkiCollab are cached by a content distribution network that stores data on servers close to you

Third-Party Sharing

  • The cards and associated media files you add to AnkiCollab are public by default.
  • We may share your data with law enforcement authorities when required to do so by law.
  • We may extract the review history of your cards to share with others for academic research purposes. This may include the contents of your cards (such as text).
  • That research use covers review history you opted in to share as well. Where we have shared review history for academic research before you withdrew consent, the copy already provided to researchers cannot be recalled, but no further data is shared, and our own stored copy is deleted.
  • As decks are contributed by other Anki users, they may contain links to third-party servers not under our control. This includes any images embedded in the deck or add-on descriptions - when you view a deck on AnkiCollab, your browser will fetch any images that are hosted on remote sites, and those remote sites will learn of your IP address and may send a tracking cookie when an image is fetched from them.

Data Retention

  • We will retain your user data while you continue to use the service. Account data may expire if the account has not been used in 6 months or more.
  • Review statistics are kept only for as long as you share them for that deck. Withdrawing sharing stops future uploads immediately and deletes the statistics already shared within 24 hours; cached statistics pages may lag by up to that long, and removing a subscription offers to delete them at the same time.
  • If you wish to remove your user data, you can delete your AnkiCollab account yourself, from your profile page - your passkeys and every device you have linked go with it. If you would rather we did it, email us at ankicollab@proton.me with your username and we will remove the account as soon as we can.
  • If you delete your account, we will delete your user data.
  • A review you performed on someone else's suggestion stays with the deck, attributed to no one, because removing it would rewrite the deck's history for everyone else. The same applies to your other contributions.
  • Daily aggregate counts for a deck - how many subscribers it had on a given day, and how many suggestions it received - are not linked to any individual. They are kept indefinitely, including after an account is deleted, because they describe the deck rather than a person.
  • Some of your user data may remain in backups and logs when your account is deleted.
  • We will retain information when legally required to do so.

Keeping Data Safe

  • We endeavor to keep your data safe by following best practices such as encrypting your data in transit, keeping machines up to date, and maintaining backups.
  • No system can ever be completely secure, so we strongly recommend you store a local copy of your user data. You can use the Export functionality in Anki clients in order to save a backup of your data.
  • As your password secures your account, please ensure you use a secure password and don't use the same password on multiple sites. If you don't already use one, we highly recommend a password manager like Bitwarden - they make life easier and keep you more secure.

Data Usage for Academic Research

  • All data provided by users on AnkiCollab, including but not limited to review history, note data, and other behavior statistics, may be used for academic research.
  • This usage is permitted as long as the privacy of users is never breached. Review statistics that you opted in to sharing are linked to your AnkiCollab account and are not anonymous: they are deleted when you withdraw consent, but copies already provided to researchers cannot be recalled.

Lawful Basis

Data processing laws require us to state the lawful basis we use for processing your data.

  • Data you provide to us such as your email address and card content is necessary for providing the service to you (servicing a contract).
  • Statistics that we collect from your data are used to improve the service, and for research (servicing a contract, and legitimate interests).
  • Review statistics you opt in to sharing are processed on the basis of your consent, which you give per deck and can withdraw at any time (see Review statistics and your rights).
  • Access logs are used for diagnostic and analytical purposes, and for detecting misuse of the service (servicing a contract, and legitimate interests).
  • Contribution records and subscription-activity records are used to credit the people who improve a deck, and to show a deck's maintainers whether their subscribers are still using it (servicing a contract, and legitimate interests).

Changes

If significant changes to this policy are made, we will notify you via a message when visiting the website, a message when you use the synchronization feature, or via email.

Transfer

In the event of a company acquisition, restructure, or bankruptcy, we may transfer your data to a different legal entity. The new legal entity shall respect your privacy in the same way, and notify you if they wish to use your data in a way not described here.
