Understanding Spaced-Repetition Lag

Why fixes take time to move deck averages.

Because Anki schedules cards on expanding intervals, editing a confusing note today only updates learner retention as subscribers encounter that card again over the coming weeks.

How cards are ranked & what spread (±σ) means

Bayesian ranking: Retention estimates are shrunk toward the deck average using the median learner count per note, so a card failed by one learner does not outrank a card consistently missed by dozens. Rows with fewer than 5 learners are dimmed as low sample.

Learner spread (±σ) & recent edits: Shown once at least 5 learners have reported stats. Low retention with tight spread means nearly everyone struggles (usually unclear phrasing or missing context); wide spread means some learners know it well while others miss a prerequisite. Cards marked Revised were edited within 60 days and may improve naturally once subscribers reach their next scheduled review.
