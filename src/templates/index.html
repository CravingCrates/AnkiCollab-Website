Welcome to AnkiCollab, the 100% free and open-source platform that lets you collaborate with others to create and share Anki decks. If you're tired of making cards alone, AnkiCollab offers a platform to work together and create comprehensive study resources.


Every deck made from scratch repeats work someone before you has already done. AnkiCollab is built around the opposite idea that the best resources come from many people refining the same thing over time, not everyone starting over alone. What one student learns gets passed forward instead of lost, so studying gets easier for everyone who comes next.
